Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Deuce Dial doesn't collect any personal data.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Your matches stay on your watch and in your own iCloud.

Deuce Dial is a tennis scorekeeping app for Apple Watch. We don't collect, store or share any of your personal data.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Match scores, history and settings

Stored on your Apple Watch. Match history is also synced through your own private iCloud account so it is available on your devices. We cannot see it.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app records each match as a Tennis workout and reads your heart rate and active calories to show them during the match. This data is stored in Apple Health on your devices and is never sent to us or anyone else.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No data sold or shared

Your choices

You can change Health access at any time in the Settings app (Health → Data Access & Devices → Deuce Dial), delete matches in the app's History, and delete the app to remove its data from your watch. iCloud data can be managed in your iCloud settings.

Questions about this policy: support@deucedial.com
