# Deuce Dial Support

Questions, bugs or feature requests? Email **[support@deucedial.com](mailto:support@deucedial.com)**.

## How do I score a point?
Turn the Digital Crown down for your point and up for your opponent's. A tap on your wrist confirms each point.

## How do I undo a point?
Swipe in from the left edge of the screen, or tap the undo button at the top left.

## How do I pause or end a match?
Swipe up to pause and go Home. Resume Match picks up where you left off. Swipe down to end the match early.

## My match history isn't on my new watch.
Make sure the watch is signed in to iCloud. After a fresh install, History can take a few minutes to download.

## Why does Deuce Dial ask for Health access?
To save each match as a Tennis workout and to show your heart rate and calories during play. The app works without it.

## Where do I see my workouts?
In the Fitness app on your iPhone or Apple Watch, listed as Tennis.
